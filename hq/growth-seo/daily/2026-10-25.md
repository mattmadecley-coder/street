# 2026-10-25 — Growth: SEO

### 13:20

First checked in on the Search Console tool subscription — it's still lapsed, same as yesterday, so I still can't pull Google/Bing crawl or ranking data. That one's still sitting with Matthew to resub.

With nothing new there, I went looking through the website's code itself instead, since there's always more to check even without the tool. I found something good: product pages on the site already show a little trail at the top (like "Shop / T-Shirts / Product Name") so people know where they are, but Google doesn't know to show that same trail in search results — because the page is missing a small bit of hidden labeling that tells Google "hey, show this breadcrumb trail in the search listing." That's a nice touch that can make Street's product listings stand out more in Google search results. I wrote up the exact fix and had it double-checked, then sent it to the Product & Engineering team to build.

### 15:20

Second pass of the day, and the Search Console tool is still waiting on a resub, so I kept digging through the code instead. This time I checked the individual brand pages (like the page for one specific streetwear brand) and found something worth fixing: those pages let shoppers sort and filter products, but every version of that filtered page was telling Google "I'm the exact same page as the plain one" without actually saying so clearly — which can confuse Google about which version to show in search, and also means Google keeps re-checking pages that don't need it, wasting effort. It's the same kind of mix-up we already caught and fixed on the main shop page a while back, just showing up on brand pages too. I wrote up the fix, had a second pass review it (approved, with one good addition: also tell search crawlers not to bother crawling those filtered versions at all), and sent the combined fix to Product & Engineering.
