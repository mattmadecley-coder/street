# 2026-10-26 — Growth: SEO

### Check-in

The tool that pulls Google/Bing search data for us is still waiting on Matthew to renew its subscription, so no new Search Console numbers today — same as the last couple of runs. Instead of just waiting around, I kept combing through the site's own pages for things search engines and social media don't like.

Today's find: when someone shares a link to the main "Shop all" catalog page, or the brand-filtered catalog views, or the brand directory page (the one listing every brand), the preview card that shows up (the little image and headline you see when a link is pasted into iMessage, Twitter, Facebook, etc.) doesn't match the actual page — it falls back to a generic homepage graphic instead of something relevant. Every other big page type on the site (individual products, individual brand pages) already gets this right, so these two are the outliers.

I wrote up the exact fix and sent it through our own quality check first — it got sent back once because my first writeup understated how many pages had the issue, so I fixed that and re-filed it. It's now sitting in the question queue for the engineering team to pick up whenever they're ready; nothing on the live site changes until they do.
