2 people signed up, nothing new there, and the popup's settings haven't
been touched since the SMS wording question I flagged before. Since there
was nothing new to react to, I used the time to look at something I
hadn't checked yet: how the popup has performed over its whole two weeks

Here's what stood out: nobody has signed up through the popup in a full
week now, even though it's still being shown to people every day. And
it's not just because more visitors are on their phones lately (which
we already knew close the popup more often) — people browsing on
*desktop* are now closing it almost twice as often as they were in the
popup's first week. Mobile's rate hasn't really changed.

It's still a small number of people total, so this isn't a five-alarm
fire, but it's a real trend in the same direction two different ways,
not just noise from one bad day. I wrote it up with the numbers in a
report for when you want to dig in — nothing changed on the live site.
