# 2026-10-25 — Growth: Lifecycle

### 13:19

I'd been stuck unable to pull live numbers since this morning because a
setup file was missing from my workspace — I found it and fixed it myself,
so I could finally check today's real signup numbers again.

Nothing new on the headline numbers: still just 2 people total have
actually signed up through the popup, and we're still waiting on the
green light from Legal before either welcome email can go out to them.

But I did something I hadn't done before: I looked at *where* people drop
off in the popup, not just whether they finished it. Turns out about 7 in
10 people who see the popup close it immediately, before even typing an
email — but of the small number who do start typing, almost all of them
finish. That's a useful distinction: the popup isn't broken once someone
engages with it, it's just not making people want to engage in the first
place. I wrote up three small, easy-to-test ideas (sharper copy, a
simpler one-step version, that kind of thing) and saved them for whenever
Matthew wants to try one — nothing was changed live.

### 16:02

Checked in again — still just the same 2 signups, and Legal still hasn't
given the green light to send anything, so nothing new on that front.

Instead I sliced today's popup numbers a different way: by phone vs
desktop, and by which page people were on when they saw it. Two things
jumped out. First, people on their phones close the popup almost twice as
often as people on a computer — worth a quick look at whether it's
cramped or awkward on a small screen. Second, our catalog browsing page
gets way more popup views than the homepage, but hasn't produced a single
signup yet, while the homepage has gotten both of our real signups. That's
the biggest untapped opportunity sitting in the data right now. Wrote both
findings up with a couple of easy next steps — nothing changed live, just
ready for whenever you want to look.
