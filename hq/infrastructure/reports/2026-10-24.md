## Issues found (filed in questions.md, not acted on)

1. **DB connection-pool exhaustion.** 120+ occurrences of `Timed out
   acquiring connection from connection pool.` across catalog/paged-
   catalog/related-products/single-product/brand lookups. The app
   catches these and returns null/empty rather than a 500, so it's
   invisible to a point-in-time curl check but means real visitors are
   probably getting degraded/empty pages some fraction of the time.
   Likely an infra-level pool-sizing issue (Postgres `max_connections`
   or PostgREST `db-pool` vs. concurrent site + cron load on a
   memory-constrained VPS) -- not something to size unilaterally.
2. **Missing `authorize_cron_watchdog` Postgres function.** Both the
   `catalog` and `storefront-health` cron routes call
   `rpc/authorize_cron_watchdog`, but no migration in
   `supabase/migrations/` ever creates it. `storefront-health`'s log
   shows the resulting "watchdog authorization failed" error explicitly;
   `catalog` likely hits the same failure without a distinct log line.
   Needs a migration -- Tier 3, flagged rather than written.
