### Self-directed goal: explained the visitors-gap known-gotcha

charter.md's Known Gotchas has carried "dashboard visitors vs raw
distinct browsers not reconciled" as an open item since the very first
briefing. Read `lib/analytics-daily.ts` and `lib/analytics-audience.ts`
(read-only, Tier 1) to actually answer it:

- `recorded_visitors` = every distinct `anonymous_user_id` seen in the
  window.
- `visitors` = the subset passing an in-memory `likelyHuman` check in
  `analyzeAudience()`: a profile is *excluded* from `visitors` if it's a
  single-page, zero-meaningful-action, single-session visit under 15
  seconds with no social attribution ("shortPassiveVisit"), or if it's
  flagged "suspected" (an impossible device fingerprint, or
  Linux+Chrome paired with a short passive non-social visit).
- This is a separate, per-query heuristic computed from raw event rows
  — it is **not** the same mechanism as the DB-level `excluded` column
  that `analytics_flagged_visitors`/`analytics_bot_rules` set. No
  double-handling risk between the two; they're independent filters
  that happen to both suppress bot-like traffic.
- This matches what's in the data: e.g. 2026-10-24 shows 51 `visitors`
  vs 53 `recorded_visitors`, a small gap of likely one-and-done bounces,
  not a tracking bug.

Updated `charter.md`'s Known Gotchas bullet with this explanation (no
longer "not reconciled") and marked the matching goal done in
`goals.md`. No code or SQL changed — this was read-only investigation
and documentation, both Tier 1.
