# October 25, 2026 — Analytics & Insights

### Afternoon check-in

Everything we track looked healthy again this time around: visitor numbers, outbound clicks to brand stores, and the handful of "is this a bot?" flags were all steady and unchanged from this morning. We also double-checked the Google Search traffic numbers we flagged earlier today — nothing new has shown up yet, so we're still waiting to hear back on that one.

With nothing new or urgent to chase, we used the time to finally solve a small long-standing puzzle: why the visitor count on the dashboard is always a few people lower than the raw number of unique browsers that showed up. Turns out it's working as intended — it's quietly filtering out one-second drive-by visits that don't look like a real person browsing (no clicks, no scrolling, gone in under 15 seconds). That's now written down clearly for the first time, so nobody has to wonder about it again.

### Evening check-in

All the usual numbers checked out clean again — same brand click counts, same two flagged suspicious visitors, nothing new. Still no word back on this morning's Google Search question. So this time we chased down a second mystery: for a while now, some search records on the site have been missing their "how many results did this search find" number. Turns out it's a small timing glitch — when someone searches, the page sometimes grabs that number a split second too early, before the real results have finished loading, so it comes back blank. We found the exact spot in the code causing it and roughly half of recent searches are affected, which also means a "zero results found" tracker elsewhere on the dashboard has been missing about half its real cases. We can't fix code ourselves, so we wrote up exactly what's wrong and handed it to the product team to patch. 🔎
