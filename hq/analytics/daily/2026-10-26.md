# 2026-10-26 — Analytics & Insights

### Morning check-in

Today's numbers looked normal across the board — site traffic, searches, and clicks to brand sites all in the usual range, nothing that looks like bots or fake traffic. 🔍

I did spot one visitor who searched "jeans" five times and browsed dozens of products over a couple of hours — at first glance that looked odd (like a bot loop), but digging in showed it was just someone genuinely shopping for jeans for a while. Good to double-check rather than assume the worst.

The more useful find: that "broken image" number that's been bouncing around in recent check-ins turned out not to be random noise after all. I traced it down to about 20 specific product pages — mostly from a handful of brands — where the photo files have gone dead on the brand's own end (moved, renamed, or deleted). It's not a site-wide problem, just a short list of products with broken pictures that real shoppers keep running into. I've flagged the exact list to the Catalog team so they can get those fixed. 📸

Also checked in again on the Google Search traffic trend — it's now three weeks running where more people are seeing Street in search results, but fewer of them click relative to how many see it. Still waiting on a response to that from last week, so just noting it's continuing rather than raising a new flag.
