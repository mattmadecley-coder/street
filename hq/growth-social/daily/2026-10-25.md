# October 25, 2026 -- Growth: Social

### 4:50 PM

Found something more serious than it first looked. For the past couple of days we knew every other Instagram carousel slot (the ones scheduled on the half-hour) was getting stuck as a draft instead of posting. Today I double-checked by looking directly at the real, live Instagram account instead of trusting our own dashboard -- and it turns out **nothing has posted to Instagram in about 21 hours**, not just the half-hour slots. The posts scheduled on the hour, which we thought were working fine, actually aren't live either.

The sneaky part: our own admin dashboard is showing those missed posts as "posted" anyway, because the system marks a post as done once its scheduled time passes, without ever checking whether it actually went out. So at a glance everything looks normal, but the Instagram account has effectively gone quiet for almost a full day.

I didn't try to fix this myself or push anything out manually -- this is a script bug that needs an engineer's attention, and I flagged it clearly for Product with everything they'll need to track it down. Everything else checked out fine today: the follow bot, Pinterest, and TikTok's stats tracking are all healthy (TikTok posting itself is still paused on purpose while we work through a separate issue there).
